# Desk Widgets

Quiet widgets for the macOS desktop, drawn in dots and ink: the time, your next meeting, three things to focus on, the weather, what's playing and how your Mac is doing. 23 widgets in six families, all in one theme you choose.

Requires **macOS 27**. Free.

## Install

1. Download **DeskWidgets-x.y.z.zip** from the [latest release](https://github.com/Swapnil-Pramanik/DeskWidgets/releases/latest).
2. Unzip it and drag **Desk Widgets** into **Applications**.
3. **First launch:** this build isn't notarised, so macOS says it can't verify the developer.
   Open the app once, then go to **System Settings › Privacy & Security**, scroll to *Security*, and click **Open Anyway**.
4. Follow the welcome tour. It shows every widget, asks for calendar, reminders and location access, and explains how to add widgets:
   right-click the desktop › **Edit Widgets** › search "Desk Widgets".

Desk Widgets lives in the **menu bar** (no Dock icon). Closing its window doesn't quit it; click the menu bar icon for Settings, Reload and Quit.

## Updating

When *Version x is available* appears in the menu bar panel or Settings › General, click **Download**, quit Desk Widgets, drag the new app into Applications (replace) and open it. Your widgets, settings, theme and quotes stay. The first launch shows what's new.

## Privacy

Everything stays on your Mac. Calendar and Reminders are read locally; the weather comes from [Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com) using your town's coordinates; the app checks this page's releases once a day for updates. No accounts, no analytics.

## Uninstall

Quit Desk Widgets from the menu bar, remove its widgets from the desktop, then delete **Desk Widgets** from Applications and the folder `~/Library/Application Support/DeskWidgets`.
